Spline interpolation, wrapping the PSPLINE / EZspline library. 

Split out of \doxylink{namespacenum__utilities}{num\+\_\+utilities} so that the (otherwise pure) numerical utilities can be compiled and unit-\/tested without the PSPLINE external dependency.
